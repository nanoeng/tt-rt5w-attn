2. ff/125 C chain error (28 LSB on 19 Nov): cause = subthreshold leakage of minimum-length switches at 125 C
   nfet_g5v0d10v5 V_GS=0, V_DS=1.5 V: W0.84/L0.5 2 pA (tt/27) vs 520 pA (ff/125) = 5 % of an IDAC unit (I_u/15);
   L1 57 pA, L2 18 pA, L4 1.4 pA (ff/125).
   - readout alone at ff: +6.5 % gain (codes up to +12); DC currents correct to 1 %; in the transient the intr
     replica dummy (255 off cascodes to vss) leaked ~100 nA and the off DAC bits on int up to ~30 nA
   - front end at DC (ideal readout): 14..19 LSB at ff vs 7..9 at tt; row currents 15..33 % high (off IDAC and
     k-scale switches)
   Fix (schematic, then layout; all DRC 0 / LVS clean):
   - IDAC and k-scale unit switches W0.84/L0.5 -> W0.42/L1 (units.SWL)
   - readout DAC switch cascodes + diode cascode L0.5 -> L1 (readout.DCASL); replica dummy source at vr
